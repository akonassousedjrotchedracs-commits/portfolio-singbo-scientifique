# Scientific profile — Akonassou Sèdjro Tchédrac Singbo

Medical biotechnologist. Hospital laboratory · biomedical research · data analysis.

Since August 2026, I have worked as a Health Action Inspector in Biomedical Analysis (IAS/ABM) in the Laboratory Department of the Mother and Child University Hospital–Lagune (CHU-MEL) in Cotonou. My practice spans haematology, clinical biochemistry, serology/immunology, microbiology and parasitology: blood smears, ELISA, immunoturbidimetry, bacterial culture, Gram staining and antimicrobial susceptibility testing.

My work is guided by analytical quality, traceability, biosafety, confidentiality and careful communication of limitations.

I hold a Professional Bachelor's degree (Licence professionnelle) in Medical Biotechnology from ENSBBA–UNSTIM (2024). My thesis, defended on 23 July 2024 and graded 17.67/20, reports a retrospective and prospective study of 81 patients at CHUD Ouémé-Plateau (total PSA, CRP and complete blood count, comparisons between PSA groups). Only aggregate results are presented; individual data remain confidential.

Tools used in the projects: standard-library Python, SQL/SQLite, data-quality checks, Wilson confidence intervals, SVG chart generation and automated tests. A Power BI dashboard specification is also presented.
