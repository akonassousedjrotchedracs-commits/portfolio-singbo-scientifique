**Medical biotechnology | Clinical microbiology | Bioinformatics | Biomedical data analysis | SQL and ETL**

I hold a degree in medical biotechnology from ENSBBA–UNSTIM in Dassa-Zou, Benin. My hospital-laboratory experience includes haematology, clinical biochemistry, serology, microbiology and parasitology. I am developing complementary skills in Python, SQL, biostatistics, data quality and visualization.

This portfolio applies a reproducible workflow: define a question, validate data structure, apply explicit transformations, generate readable outputs, check consistency and discuss limitations. It contains four projects: descriptive antimicrobial-resistance analysis, basic sequence quality control, SQL/ETL data modelling and statistical biomedical analysis.

Source code, demonstration datasets and reports are included for inspection and reproducibility. Professional experience is distinguished from independent portfolio work; projects in this repository are not represented as tools deployed at the hospital. Demonstration data are labelled as such and are not patient records or a public surveillance export.
