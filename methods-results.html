Methods and results

A cross-project view of quality controls, calculations, figures and limitations.

Shared rule

Each analysis starts with a question and a definition of the population or unit of observation. Fields are checked before calculation; denominators are retained; outputs include interpretation and limits.

By domain

  • Microbiology: S/I/R categories, counts and proportions.
  • Bioinformatics: FASTA format, quality checks and sequence metrics.
  • Laboratory data: relational model, ETL, audit and provenance.
  • Biostatistics: distributions, proportions, intervals and uncertainty.
  • Thesis defense: PSA, CBC, CRP and prostate inflammation.
