Methods and results

Reading notes for outputs, denominators and limitations.

Data status: figures in projects 01 to 04 come from synthetic datasets. Project 05 is the undergraduate thesis and is kept separate from the demonstrations.

Demonstration projects

  • 01 · Antimicrobial resistance: 100 synthetic observations, ten organism–antibiotic pairs, 10 tests per pair; S/I/R counts remain separate.
  • 02 · Sequence quality control: three synthetic FASTA sequences; length, GC fraction, ambiguous bases and exploratory ORF indicator.
  • 03 · SQL and ETL: 191 synthetic observations, value checks, aggregations and provenance manifest.
  • 04 · Statistical analysis: six synthetic groups (50 observations each), proportions and 95% Wilson intervals.

Thesis

05 · Evaluation of inflammation in prostatic dysfunction: thesis document dated 23 July 2024, Medical Biotechnology programme (ENSBBA–UNSTIM), reporting 81 participants at CHUD-OP; total PSA, CRP and CBC. Individual-level data are not provided.

Reading rules

  • Read each numerator with its denominator.
  • An untested antibiotic is not a susceptible result.
  • Do not interpret synthetic fixtures as clinical frequencies.
  • A software check or file hash does not establish biological provenance.
  • Keep the undergraduate thesis distinct from technical portfolio demonstrations.
