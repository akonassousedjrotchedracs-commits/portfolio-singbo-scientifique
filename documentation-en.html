Professional background

I hold a Professional Bachelor's degree (Licence professionnelle) in Medical Biotechnology (ENSBBA–UNSTIM, 2024). Since August 2026, I have worked as a Health Action Inspector in Biomedical Analysis (IAS/ABM) in the CHU-MEL Laboratory Department. My experience covers haematology, clinical biochemistry, serology/immunology, microbiology and parasitology.

Projects and data status

Projects 01 to 04 are technical demonstrations using synthetic datasets. They illustrate microbiology, sequence quality checks, SQL/ETL and biostatistics. They do not represent work performed at CHU-MEL, and their numbers are not clinical findings.

Project 05 presents the thesis defended on 23 July 2024: a study of 81 participants at CHUD-OP covering total PSA, CRP and CBC (grade: 17.67/20). Only aggregate results are presented.
