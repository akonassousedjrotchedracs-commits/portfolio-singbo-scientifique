Professional background

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Medical Biotechnology from ENSBBA–UNSTIM, awarded with Honours, and have worked at CHU-MEL since July 2026. My experience covers haematology, clinical biochemistry, serology/immunology, microbiology and parasitology.

Projects and data status

Projects 01 to 04 are technical demonstrations using synthetic datasets. They illustrate microbiology, sequence quality checks, SQL/ETL and biostatistics. They do not represent work performed at CHU-MEL, and their numbers are not clinical findings.

Project 05 presents the undergraduate thesis topic: a study of 81 patients at CHUD Ouémé-Plateau covering CRP, PSA, leucocytes and cytokines. No patient identifiers or individual results are included in the public presentation.
