# Project 05 — Thesis: Inflammatory Biomarkers in Prostatic Dysfunction

**Thesis for the Professional Bachelor's degree (Licence professionnelle) in Medical Biotechnology (ENSBBA–UNSTIM)**, defended on 23 July 2024 and conducted at CHUD-OP. The study covers 81 patients aged 40 or older: 61 retrospective records and 20 participants in the prospective component. The described analyses cover total PSA, CRP and CBC; no cytokine measurement is reported. Only aggregate results are presented; individual data remain confidential. Thesis grade: 17.67/20.

This thesis is separate from synthetic demonstration projects 01–04. The public presentation must not expose patient identifiers or individual-level results.
