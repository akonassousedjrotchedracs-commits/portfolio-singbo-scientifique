# Project 05 — Thesis: Inflammatory Biomarkers in Prostatic Dysfunction

**Professional-degree thesis in the Medical Biotechnology programme (ENSBBA–UNSTIM)**, defended on 23 July 2024 and conducted at CHUD-OP. The supplied document reports 81 patients aged 40 or older: 61 retrospective records and 20 participants in the prospective component. The described analyses cover total PSA, CRP and CBC; no cytokine measurement is reported. Results are aggregated and individual-level data are unavailable, so the original statistical calculations cannot be reproduced. A mark of 17.67/20 appears in the repository but not in the supplied thesis.

This thesis is separate from synthetic demonstration projects 01–04. The public presentation must not expose patient identifiers or individual-level results.
