Biomedical research

My thesis in the Medical Biotechnology programme (ENSBBA–UNSTIM), defended on 23 July 2024, examined inflammation in prostatic dysfunction at CHUD-OP. The document reports 81 participants and analyses of total PSA, CRP and CBC. It reports a statistically significant difference for lymphocytes between groups. The thesis was graded 17.67/20. Only aggregate results are presented; individual data remain confidential.

Careful scientific interpretation. Results are reported as presented in the thesis, with their limitations (modest sample, single site). No cytokine measurement is reported.
