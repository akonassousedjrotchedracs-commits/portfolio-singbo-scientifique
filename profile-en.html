Biomedical research

My undergraduate thesis at CHUD Ouémé-Plateau examines the statistical evaluation of inflammatory biomarkers in prostatic dysfunction. The aggregate study covers 81 patients and considers CRP, PSA, leucocytes and cytokines. The thesis received a mark of 17.67/20.

Careful scientific interpretation. Portfolio results use the aggregate statistics available. Without the anonymized individual-level table, exact p-values, confidence intervals and correlations are not recalculated or added.
